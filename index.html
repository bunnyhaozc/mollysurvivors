About Molly Survivors

Molly Survivors is an action-packed survival game where you control a brave character fighting against endless waves of monsters. Your goal is simple: survive as long as possible while collecting experience, upgrading abilities, and defeating increasingly difficult enemies.

Inspired by popular roguelike survival games, Molly Survivors offers an addictive gameplay loop that keeps you coming back for "just one more run." With each playthrough, you'll discover new strategies, unlock powerful combinations, and push your survival skills to the limit.

The game features procedurally generated enemy waves, ensuring that no two games are exactly alike. As time progresses, the challenge intensifies, testing your reflexes and strategic thinking.

Survival Tips

Keep Moving

Standing still is a death sentence. Constantly move to avoid being surrounded by enemies and to collect experience gems.

Prioritize Upgrades

Choose your upgrades wisely. Focus on a few powerful abilities rather than spreading your upgrades too thin across many different weapons.
