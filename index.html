About Molly Survivors

Molly Survivors is an action-packed monster survival game where you control a brave hero fighting against endless waves of creatures. The longer you survive, the stronger the enemies become.

As you defeat monsters, you'll earn experience points that allow you to level up and acquire new weapons, abilities, and passive skills. Strategic choices about which upgrades to select will greatly impact your survival chances.

The game features beautiful graphics, smooth controls, and an addictive gameplay loop that will keep you coming back for "just one more run" to beat your previous high score!

Game Highlights

  • Variety of enemies with different attack patterns
  • Numerous weapons and power-ups to discover
  • Challenging difficulty that increases over time
  • Multiple character builds and strategies to explore
